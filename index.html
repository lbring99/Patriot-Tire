A Family-Owned Shop, Built for This Town

Patriot Tire has spent more than eight years keeping Laramie — and the ranchers, commuters, and travelers passing through on I-80 — safely on the road. We specialize in tire repair and service, but we also carry a full range of new and used tires, including up to 14-ply trailer tires for the trucks and rigs this region depends on.

We're not a chain, and we don't treat you like a number. Whether you're planning ahead or you just rolled in on a spare, our goal is the same: honest work, fair pricing, and a shop you can count on the next time, too.

OUR MISSION

To provide Laramie with high-quality, dependable tires and honest service — building lasting relationships, keeping our neighbors safe on the road, and giving back to the community we're part of.
